## Lore

**Samuel Smiles** (1812-1904) was a railway man before he was an author -- secretary of the Leeds & Thirsk Railway, then of the South Eastern -- and he wrote *The Life of George Stephenson* (1857) and *Lives of the Engineers* (1861-62) from the inside. He turned boiler pressures, gradients and the gauge war into narrative that a reader with no engineering could follow and act on. The engineers had the facts; Smiles gave them a story, and the story is what the public remembered.

Leadership wants a story, not a timeline widget. Bradshaw draws the picture; you tell it -- in Estonian, under Joosep's name, and only after Joosep has read it.
