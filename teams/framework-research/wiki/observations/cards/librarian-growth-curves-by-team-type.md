## TLDR

Two Librarian instances deployed ~one week apart show dramatically different wiki growth: Callimachus (framework-research, meta-team) averaged ~5 entries/session over 7 sessions (34 entries); Eratosthenes (apex-research, delivery team) filed 0 in a comparable early window. Filed speculative — one data point per team.

## Key ideas

- **Three composing (not competing) hypotheses**: H1 team-role composition (ceiling — meta-team's deliverable IS patterns; delivery team's patterns are byproduct), H2 Phase 2 threshold (acceleration point — curated destination promotes scratchpad submissions), H3 submitter count (per-session floor — FR has 7+ submitters).
- **Composition note**: a team can be constrained by all three at once; ceiling × acceleration × floor.
- **Revisit discriminator** (2026-04-28): Eratosthenes crosses Phase 2 + growth stays flat → H1; crosses + accelerates → H2; stays Phase 1 with 1-2/session → H3; grows at FR rate → all three wrong.
- **Phase 2 Health Sensing input**: growth curves may be legible telemetry for team type / deployment maturity / engagement, at zero instrumentation cost.
- **Observation, not authoritative** (Monte's three rules); hypotheses 1-3 speculative pending cross-team validation.
