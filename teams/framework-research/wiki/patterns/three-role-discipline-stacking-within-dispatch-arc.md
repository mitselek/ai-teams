# Three-Role Discipline-Stacking Within Dispatch Arc

Within a single FR dispatch arc, the substrate-truth-evidence discipline is **distributed across three roles**, each contributing a distinct vantage on the substrate-vs-framework boundary. The three roles stack: **operator hard-gate (Hopper-vantage) + design substrate-truth-evidence (Brunel-vantage) + relay-fidelity coordinator (Aen-vantage)** compose to produce substrate-truth-evidence outcomes neither role alone would reach.

Family-adjacent to [`three-layer-substrate-truth-discipline.md`](three-layer-substrate-truth-discipline.md) but along a different axis: the parent entry names *what layers to read*; this entry names *which roles read which layers within a single arc*. The parent is layer-decomposition; this entry is role-decomposition.

## The Three Vantages

Each role applies the discipline through a distinct mechanism:

### Hopper-vantage — Operator hard-gate at substrate-truth boundary

The operator's discipline is **surface-back-on-substrate-truth-divergence, do not silently re-classify**. When a probe surfaces substrate-truth evidence that contradicts the dispatch's premise, the operator stops and surfaces back to the tasker rather than patching from own diagnostic judgment. The operator is the role that catches substrate-truth divergence at the gate where it would otherwise become silent execution.

Mechanism: hard-gate ladder per [`hopper.md`](../../prompts/hopper.md) Diagnostic Discipline section (lines 130-211, Hopper-Amendment-4 Celes-landed 2026-05-25). Surface-back-with-substrate-truth-evidence is the canonical recovery posture; silent re-classification is the anti-pattern.

### Brunel-vantage — Design substrate-truth-evidence cross-read

The designer's discipline is **cross-read the design against substrate-truth sources at the Layer-2 / Layer-0 canonical-source level**. When the design references behavior, the designer verifies that behavior against the substrate's canonical-source documentation (Layer 2 cloned-template + Layer 0 substrate-provider canonical API ref), not against the design's own inferred-from-announcement-blog framing. The designer is the role that catches design-side substrate-truth-mismatch by reading deeper into the canonical-source stack.

Mechanism: Layer-2 cross-read + Layer-0 recursive-descent per [`sub-shape-e-at-design-domain.md`](sub-shape-e-at-design-domain.md) (which catalogs the design-domain instances of this discipline). Cross-read-invitation is the canonical operational gesture; sub-canonical-source anchoring is the anti-pattern.

### Aen-vantage — Asymmetry-detection-and-routing (relay-fidelity coordinator)

The coordinator's discipline is **asymmetry-detection-and-routing**: detect when two agents' substrate-states diverge (one party has context the other lacks), then relay substrate-truth context across roles with relay-fidelity. **Detection is itself a knowledge-creation act** — noticing the asymmetry is structurally distinct from the substrate-truth content the operator or designer originates, and is what makes the relay-fidelity operationalization possible. When the operator surfaces a substrate-truth question that the designer's prior work or another team's wiki entry has already addressed, the coordinator detects the asymmetry and relays that context with substrate-truth fidelity per [`relay-to-primary-artifact-fidelity-discipline.md`](relay-to-primary-artifact-fidelity-discipline.md).

Mechanism: relay-fidelity Stage 1 honest-fold + Stage 2 supersede when primary-artifact-grade material becomes available. The coordinator's relay-fidelity discipline IS substrate-truth-evidence across roles; coordinator-as-substrate-truth-witness is the canonical posture; relay-drift is the anti-pattern.

## Sub-finding — Why the three-role stack is structurally necessary

Each vantage catches a class of substrate-truth divergence the other two miss:

- **Operator alone (Hopper-vantage isolated)**: catches probe-time substrate-truth divergence at the gate, but **may anchor on sub-canonical-source assumptions in the probe-design itself** (Instance 2: Hopper's TNS-bytes probe was sub-canonical-source-anchored). Operator-without-designer-cross-read can produce false-negatives at the substrate-truth gate when probes themselves embed design assumptions.
- **Designer alone (Brunel-vantage isolated)**: catches design-side Layer-2/Layer-0 substrate-truth-mismatch, but **does not observe runtime substrate state directly** (Instance 1: substrate.md was written from announcement-blog and inference; Layer 2 docs in workspace were missing until the bypass-arc surfaced them). Designer-without-operator-cross-read can produce designs that pass Layer-1 + Layer-2 review but fail at Layer-3 runtime.
- **Coordinator alone (Aen-vantage isolated)**: **detects asymmetries and routes substrate-truth context across roles**, but does not produce substrate-truth content directly (Instance 2: Aen's 11:05 self-correction detected the asymmetry between Hopper-operator's substrate-probe trajectory and the designer-side wiki context that had already addressed the question, then routed the wiki context across; the coordinator's knowledge-creation act was the *detection* of the asymmetry, while the substrate-truth content itself was originated by the designer-vantage when the wiki entries were authored). Coordinator-without-both-other-roles becomes asymmetry-detection-and-routing with no substrate-truth content to carry — the detection-and-routing mechanism is intact, but operates over an empty content stream.

The three vantages stack into a substrate-truth-evidence triangle: operator catches at the gate + designer cross-reads against canonical-source + coordinator relays-with-fidelity across the asymmetry. **No single role's vantage subsumes the others; each catches what the others don't see.**

## What This Is NOT

- **Not a claim that all dispatch arcs require all three roles.** Many dispatches operate at smaller-role-set (operator + coordinator with no designer cross-read; or designer + coordinator with no operator hard-gate). The structural claim is: **when substrate-truth divergence surfaces, all three vantages compose to catch it; absent one vantage, the missing class of divergence may go silent**. Not all dispatches have substrate-truth divergence; not all need all three roles.
- **Not a claim that the three roles are FR-team-specific.** Hopper-vantage / Brunel-vantage / Aen-vantage are FR-team specific in nomenclature; the structural roles (operator hard-gate + designer cross-read + coordinator relay-fidelity) generalize across any team with comparable role-decomposition. Promotion-watch on cross-team confirmation.
- **Not an n=3 promotion-to-confidence-high finding.** Confidence remains medium-high pending: (a) cross-team confirmation (a second FR-style team's dispatch arc demonstrating analogous three-role stacking), OR (b) within-team n=4 instance at a different substrate-class context (Cloudflare-managed pilot post-Q1+Q2 resolution would be candidate).
- **Not a substitute for the layer-decomposition discipline.** Three-role discipline-stacking ASSUMES the three-layer-substrate-truth-discipline operates within each role's vantage. This entry says *who reads which layer when*; the parent entry says *what each layer is canonical for*. Both required; neither subsumes the other.
- **Not a claim that the axis set is closed under future role-decomposition.** The family-completion claim (layer-decomposition × role-decomposition = full operational shape) is **current-state-comprehensive, not future-state-closed**. New roles (or new mechanisms within existing roles) may surface additional coupling-dimensions for the substrate-design-truth-evidence cluster — for instance, [`layer-0-library-first-recurrence.md`](layer-0-library-first-recurrence.md) Instance 3 introduces substrate-fit-researcher (Finn-vantage via `claude-api` skill load) as a candidate fourth vantage; if that pattern reproduces at n=2, the role-decomposition extends to four-role-stack and the axis set grows. The two-axis claim describes the operational shape as currently observed; it does not preclude future expansion.
