## TLDR

When two specialists working from orthogonal starting questions independently arrive at the same structural shape — and neither side needs rework on the other's deliverable to accommodate it — that is lossless independent convergence. It is stronger evidence the shape is emergent (not a shared blind spot artifact) than post-discussion convergence.

## Key ideas

- **"Lossless" is load-bearing**: the deliverables compose cleanly as-shipped, no edits needed for compatibility — distinct from worked-it-out convergence where edges get shaved to fit.
- **Diagnostic question**: if you removed the discussion that produced the convergence, would the deliverables still compose? Lossless = yes.
- **Three joint conditions**: orthogonal starting questions, independent first ships, no-rework integration.
- **Why stronger**: it proves the shape is reachable from independent vantages without coordination — a property of the problem, not the team's protocol.
- **Timing matters**: the lossless property is observed at first cross-read, not after iteration (silent rework can make lossy look lossless).
- **Not "we agree"** (alignment vs shape) and **not validation of correctness** (could be a shared blind spot the convergence makes visible).
