## TLDR

Repeated substrate-truth-evidence passes across a single document's authoring trajectory each narrow the document's substrate-blind-spots, but no single pass eliminates them. Recursive-narrowing is a property of the discipline itself -- identity-agnostic on reviewer -- not an artifact of how an author drafts or who reviews. Substrate-truth-evidence is asymptotic, not terminal.

## Key ideas

- **Not "cross-team review catches things"** (a weaker social claim) -- within a single author's frame, repeated passes successively narrow the blind-spot set; the discipline is identity-agnostic on reviewer.
- **Within-author n=5** (Herald's comms.md v1.0→v1.4) is the structural-sufficient evidence; **cross-author n=4** is the reproducibility-necessary corroboration. Sufficient × necessary, not redundant.
- **Each pass's catch is enabled BY the previous pass's tightening** -- causal, not just temporally posterior; the next blind-spot lives one layer deeper.
- **Substrate-knowledge co-determination**: each catcher caught what their substrate-knowledge enabled given the version they read -- recursive-narrowing is a (framing × knowledge) joint configuration.
- **Operational posture**: do not treat "passed review at version N" as "complete at version N" -- version N+1 will likely surface a deeper blind-spot.
- **n=5 meta-instance**: the discipline catches the blind-spot in the document articulating the discipline (within-system self-application, held at sketch-grade).
