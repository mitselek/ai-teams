## TLDR

When two specialists exchange `[COORDINATION]` messages on a contract design, the loop itself can produce within-specialist self-corrections at coordination-tempo (single exchange), not session-tempo. The cross-specialist's structurally-better reshape becomes the trigger for the catcher's second-pass re-read of their own framing.

## Key ideas

- **The shape**: A ships v1, B reshapes own work touching A's framing, A re-reads own v1 in that light, A's framing now reads as the symmetric mistake A would have caught, A retracts.
- **Two anti-shapes it avoids**: performative agreement (absorb without re-reading own v1) and adversarial defense (defend v1 to avoid loss-of-face).
- **Discipline = treat your own v1 as third-party material**; apply the same structural test you'd apply to a stranger's design; cite the structural argument, not the social compromise.
- **Reshaper requirement**: apply the improvement to your OWN work first ("I had this wrong"), not as a critique; don't expect the catcher to retract -- let them.
- **Sub-shape A** (prior-cross-specialist-argument-as-trigger): retract using an argument a peer made in a PRIOR session, internalized as a self-test.
- **Sub-shape B** (pre-commit-to-extension irony): a pre-commit that didn't land was still load-bearing scaffolding for the better commitment.
- **n=2 within one 5-message loop** (Prism deliverable B + Surface 2); watch n=3 across loops for promotion.
