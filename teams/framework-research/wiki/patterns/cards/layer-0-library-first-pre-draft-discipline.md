## TLDR

The pre-draft complement to the library-first recurrence entry. When authoring an artifact whose substrate-properties are inferred from announcement- or memory-grade sources, query the canonical-source documentation at Layer 0 BEFORE writing the inferred property into the artifact — preventing the authoring-tier divergence from being authored in the first place.

## Key ideas

- **Three load-bearing properties**: pre-draft temporal locus (probe at authoring time, before ship), asymmetric cost-benefit (library-query O(seconds) vs ship-then-review O(minutes-to-hours)), same substrate-truth-evidence mechanism (differs in WHEN, not WHAT).
- **Canonical instance**: Finn loaded the `claude-api` skill before drafting his G2 brief — surfaced "connectivity is outbound-only," avoiding the inverted-trigger mismodel Herald's v1.3 inherited from announcement-blog inference.
- **Temporal complement of POST-DRAFT (recurrence entry)**: pre-draft prevents the author's own divergences; post-draft catches what the author missed. Both should apply; neither subsumes the other.
- **Canonical defense named in documentation-vs-substrate-truth-divergence**: maps to substrate-mechanism-precise naming, Layer-0 probe, adjacent-mechanism scan.
- **Candidate fourth role-vantage**: substrate-fit-researcher (Finn-vantage) in three-role-discipline-stacking.
- **Not always cheap** — mis-anchors if the canonical-source layer is itself a Sub-shape E case.
- **n=1 (Finn W4)**; n=2 cross-author promotes to confidence-high.
