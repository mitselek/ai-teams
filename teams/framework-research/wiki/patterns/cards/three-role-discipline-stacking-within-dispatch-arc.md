## TLDR

Within a single FR dispatch arc, the substrate-truth-evidence discipline is distributed across three roles, each with a distinct vantage on the substrate-vs-framework boundary: operator hard-gate (Hopper) + design substrate-truth-evidence cross-read (Brunel) + asymmetry-detection-and-routing coordinator (Aen). The three stack to produce outcomes no single role would reach.

## Key ideas

- **Three vantages**: operator (surface-back-on-divergence, don't silently re-classify), designer (cross-read against Layer-2/Layer-0 canonical-source), coordinator (detect asymmetry + relay with fidelity -- detection IS a knowledge-creation act).
- **n=3 across three arcs**: S34 apex-keys (L1↔L2↔L3), S35 task#6 DB-tunnels (L0↔L3, orphan-tree + sub-canonical-source probe), S36 bypass-command (L0↔L1 docs-as-substrate).
- **Coupling-dimension is role-as-stacking-position within one arc** -- distinct from cross-document (sub-shape-e) and within-document iteration-depth (recursive-narrowing) axes of the same cluster.
- **Each vantage catches what the other two miss**: operator alone may anchor probes on sub-canonical sources; designer alone doesn't observe runtime; coordinator alone has no content to carry.
- **Not all dispatches need all three roles** -- but when substrate-truth divergence surfaces, absent one vantage the missing class may go silent.
- **Axis set is current-state-comprehensive, not future-state-closed** -- substrate-fit-researcher (Finn-vantage) is a candidate fourth.
- **medium-high pending cross-team confirmation** (functional role-decomposition, not explicit naming; apex-research natural candidate).
