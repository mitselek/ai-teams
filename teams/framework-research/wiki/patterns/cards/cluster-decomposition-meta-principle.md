## TLDR

Clusters decompose along their coupling-dimension; identifying that dimension is the load-bearing first analytical move when observing any cluster. Each component maps to exactly one distinguishable property of the system. Clean mapping = the cluster decomposes into sibling-positions along the dimension; otherwise it is monolithic along that axis.

## Key ideas

- **The test**: identify the system, identify a candidate coupling-dimension, test the mapping -- one-to-one means decomposition, many-to-one/one-to-many means wrong dimension or monolithic.
- **Naming the dimension is the structural move**: components stop being a list and become positions along an axis the dimension itself becomes analyzable.
- **n=3 across three dimensions**: mVox M1-M5 (team-property), Cloudflare 7-mechanism (team-property, different range), Sub-shape E three-layer (ownership-locus).
- **Meta-principle is "clusters decompose along THEIR coupling-dimension"** -- not always team-property; the first move is to identify which dimension.
- **Methodology corollary**: decompositions are invisible at n=1, emerge at n=2 when a second instance supplies variation. Single-instance cluster observation is the wrong unit of analysis.
- **Operational dual is bottleneck-determines-adoption (C4)**: C1 names the axis, C4 picks the position -- sequential.
- **n=4 with a novel fourth coupling-dimension promotes to confidence-high**; two candidate fourth-axes (failure-semantics, lifecycle-phase) on standing watch.
