# Sub-Shape E at the Design Domain -- Pattern Catches Its Own Authors

The three-layer substrate-truth discipline ([`three-layer-substrate-truth-discipline.md`](three-layer-substrate-truth-discipline.md)) was authored from the S34 apex-keys arc on FR-shipped Docker substrates. On 2026-05-26, within a single ~2-hour window, **four independent exec-readiness reviews of FR's own Cloudflare-pilot design surfaced Sub-shape E at four different layer-pair-points of the design-domain itself** -- the discipline catching its own authors at the design layer it was authored to catch operators at.

This is family-adjacent to [`cluster-decomposition-meta-principle.md`](cluster-decomposition-meta-principle.md) Origin 3 (three-layer model along ownership-locus axis), generalized one level further: **the design domain is itself a substrate that has its own three-layer structure**, and drift surfaces between FR's design-as-shipped vs consumer-of-substrate-canonical-source vs runtime-evidence-on-deployed-state can be observed AND named with the same discipline that was authored to catch them at the substrate runtime layer.

**The recursive structure is the headline.** The three-layer model surfaced from S34 apex-keys arc (operator-runtime layer); the same model now applies one level up to FR's own design work (designer-runtime layer); the discipline catches its own authors at the layer where they were drafting the discipline that catches operators.

## Recursive-Elegance -- The Discipline Catching Its Own Authors

The three-layer substrate-truth discipline was authored by Brunel + Hopper from the S34 apex-keys arc on FR-shipped Docker substrates (operator-runtime layer). It was promoted to canonical via [`Hopper-Amendment-4`](../../prompts/hopper.md#diagnostic-discipline--three-layer-substrate-truth-reading-before-executing) (Celes-landed 2026-05-25, prompt lines 130-211).

**Within 36 hours of the amendment landing, four independent exec-readiness reviews surfaced Sub-shape E at four distinct layer-pair-points of FR's own Cloudflare-pilot design** -- including (Sub-Instance 3) Brunel's own design work being caught by his own discipline, and (Sub-Instance 4) Brunel + Hopper's joint bypass-arc producing a Layer-0-recursive-descent extension of the discipline they had just promoted to canonical.

The recursive structure has three load-bearing properties:

1. **The discipline applies at the layer above where it was authored.** The S34 catalyzing arc was at operator-runtime layer; the S36 surfacings are at designer-runtime layer. Same shape, layer up.
2. **The discipline catches its own authors.** Brunel-as-designer was caught by Brunel-as-discipline-author. Hopper-as-correction-attempter was caught by Brunel-as-cross-reader. No one is exempt from the layer-pair drift the discipline names.
3. **The discipline extends recursively at its own canonical-source layer.** Sub-Instance 4 generalizes the discipline from substrate-as-runtime to docs-as-substrate. The substrate's own documentation has its own layer structure; library-first descends through doc-provenance layers when mechanism-ambiguity exists. **The discipline produces an extension of itself by being applied to its own application.**

This is family-adjacent to [`first-use-recursive-validation.md`](first-use-recursive-validation.md) and [`recursive-citation-as-canonical-validation.md`](recursive-citation-as-canonical-validation.md) -- both within-system self-application patterns. **The within-system distinction here is sharp: the discipline's application produces evidence for the discipline's own load-bearing property at the layer-up domain.** First-use-recursive-validation caught a rule violating itself within the same domain; this entry catches the rule's application surfacing instances of itself at a layer above the rule's original domain.

## Sub-Instance 4 as Docs-as-Substrate Extension

Sub-Instance 4 specifically warrants framing as an extension of the three-layer discipline rather than as a fourth instance of the same shape. Sub-Instances 1–3 share the layer-pair shape (FR-design L1 vs CF-canonical-doc L2 vs deployed-runtime L3); Sub-Instance 4 descends one level further:

- **Within Layer 2 of Sub-Instances 1–3** (the CF-canonical-doc layer) there is itself a layer structure: CF's product/consumer-of-its-own-API docs (Layer 1 of substrate-provider documentation, UI-focused phrasing) vs CF's API-canonical reference (Layer 0 of substrate-provider documentation, mechanism-canonical).
- **The bypass-arc on 2026-05-26 14:34–14:48 surfaced drift at this nested layer pair** -- Layer 1 of substrate-provider-docs vs Layer 0 of substrate-provider-API.
- **Library-first / canonical-source-first discipline recursively descends through doc-provenance layers** when consumer-team docs framing is mechanism-ambiguous. The cross-read invitation pattern (Brunel 14:42) is the operator-defense at the docs-provenance layer.

**The extension is not separately filed because it composes structurally with the three-layer discipline.** Future operators reading Sub-Instances 1–3 surface drift at the FR-design vs CF-canonical-doc layer; Sub-Instance 4 surfaces drift one level deeper, at the CF-product-doc vs CF-canonical-API layer. The discipline's application descends naturally; the cross-read invitation is the recovery posture at any layer.
