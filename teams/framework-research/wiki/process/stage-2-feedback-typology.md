# Stage-2 Feedback Typology — Five Empirical Shapes

When a wiki entry is filed via Protocol A Stage 1 honest-fold (drafter folds from primary-artifact source-of-truth, ships, then surfaces specific Stage 2 read-back surfaces to joint authors), the joint authors' Stage 2 responses cluster into **distinct epistemic shapes** that warrant different fold disciplines. The shapes are not interchangeable: folding a Shape-A response with Shape-B discipline (or vice versa) over-extends or under-credits the feedback systematically.

The typology catalogs five empirical shapes from S35-S36 cross-cycle observation. **Joint Callimachus + Volta** — Cal observed the typology emerging during the C1 Stage 2 cycle 2026-05-26; Volta sharpened the discriminator to a single question (does the proposal preserve the existing claim-base or extend it?) and contributed Shape-C as a third epistemic shape between A and B. Five shapes now catalogued; n=1 each within the S36 session; n=2 cross-cycle is the reproducibility-to-stable-typology promotion trigger.

## The Discriminator Question (Volta-canonical)

For any Stage 2 response, the **single-question test** is:

> **Does the proposal preserve the existing claim-base or extend it?**

The answer routes to one of two epistemic regimes:

- **Preserves claim-base** → fold-discipline (sharpens existing correct content; cheap to fold; no n-threshold required).
- **Extends claim-base** → defer-discipline (forward-claim-extension; requires n=2 of meta-principle-instances before folding; honors the methodology corollary from [`cluster-decomposition-meta-principle.md`](../patterns/cluster-decomposition-meta-principle.md) — *do not lean harder at n=1*).

The discriminator is the canonical routing question. The five empirical shapes below are sub-categories of the two routes — three preserve-claim-base sub-shapes and two on the boundary or distinct from the claim-base axis entirely.

## The Five Shapes

### Shape-A — Pure renaming-of-cleavage (preserves claim-base)

A Stage 2 proposal that **renames** a cleavage in the entry to a sharper or more legible label, preserving the existing claim-base completely. No claim is added, weakened, or extended; the framing of an existing claim becomes more precise.

**Fold discipline**: **fold immediately**. The fold sharpens correct content; no n-threshold required; entry is strictly improved.

**Empirical instances (S35-S36)**:
- Volta C1 Surface 3 (2026-05-26): within-system self-application (recursive-validation patterns) vs cross-system comparative emergence (n=1→n=2 corollary) — renames the cleavage between two sibling-classes of evidence. Folded immediately.
- Aen 2.7 Surface 3 (2026-05-26): coordinator-vantage structural contribution renamed from "surfaces asymmetries" to "asymmetry-detection-and-routing" — detection IS a knowledge-creation act; routing operationalizes the detection. Folded immediately.
- Hopper 2.5 Refinement 2 (2026-05-26): phrasing-nit precision fix on Sub-Instance 4 attribution. Folded immediately.

**n=3 within S36 alone**; cross-cycle reproducibility expected at n=5+ on subsequent Stage 2 cycles.

### Shape-B — Forward-claim-extension defer (extends claim-base)

A Stage 2 proposal that **extends** the entry's claim-base into adjacent territory — usually as a "lean harder" framing that strengthens a load-bearing claim at the current evidence base. The proposal is structurally correct AND substantively over-extends at the current n-count.

**Fold discipline**: **defer**. Honor the methodology corollary from cluster-decomposition-meta-principle.md — recursive-meta framings, family-extension claims, and lean-harder framings earn weight at n=2 of meta-principle-instances; folding at n=1 over-extends the entry's evidence base. The deferred framing lives in the scratchpad as a future-amendment candidate; if n=2 of the relevant meta-principle-instance surfaces, the fold becomes promotable.

**Empirical instances (S35-S36)**:
- Brunel C1 Surface 3 lean-harder (2026-05-26): recursive-meta framing on the n=1→n=2 corollary ("each fresh coupling-dimension validates the meta-principle's range; meta-principles validate themselves recursively along their own axes"). Brunel explicitly framed as not-a-change-request; deferred until n=2 of meta-principle-instances.
- Brunel C4 structural-detector watchpoint (2026-05-26): forward-claim-extension to a structural-detector that flags candidate sub-findings; deferred to next session+ when n=2 surfaces.

**n=2 within S36 alone**; the defer-discipline IS the correct response — folding now over-extends.

### Shape-C — Mechanism-sharpening-within-claim-base (preserves claim-base, extends mechanism explanation) — Volta-contribution

A Stage 2 proposal that **preserves the existing claim-base** (the entry's "what" stays unchanged) but **extends the mechanism explanation** (the entry's "why" gains depth). This is a third epistemic shape between Shape-A (pure renaming) and Shape-B (claim-base extension): no renaming AND no claim-base extension, but new mechanism-content explaining why the claim holds.

**Fold discipline**: **fold immediately**, with explicit mechanism-explanation labeling. The fold strengthens the entry's *why* without changing the entry's *what*; n-threshold is not the same as Shape-B (no claim-extension). The discriminator question's answer is "preserves claim-base while extending mechanism explanation."

**Empirical instances (S35-S36)**:
- Volta C4 Surface 4 (2026-05-26): proposed that the mechanism by which least-distinctive components map to common-across-teams team-properties is **"bottlenecks cluster around common-across-teams properties because common-across-teams properties are exactly the properties ALL teams interact with."** The claim-base (least-distinctive-but-highest-leverage holds across domains) is unchanged; the mechanism (why) is added. Folded immediately into C4 §Sub-finding + amendments-log + index entry.

**n=1 within S36**; promoted to typology entry on Volta-canonical observation that L1 discriminator decomposes along *claim-base* vs *mechanism explanation* axes (pure Shape-A preserves both; Shape-B extends claim-base; Shape-C preserves claim-base + extends mechanism).

### Shape-D — Factual-correction-on-inferred-content-where-FLAG-invited-correction (epistemic-layer distinct from claim-base axis)

A Stage 2 proposal that **corrects an inferred specific** that the drafter explicitly flagged as `[FLAG]`-grade inference (per Stage 1 honest-fold discipline: when the drafter cannot verify a specific from primary-artifact source-of-truth, the inference is flagged as such, inviting correction). The correction is at the **substantive-content-verification** layer, distinct from the framing-quality layer that produces Shape-A/B/C.

**Fold discipline**: **fold immediately AND strengthens the entry** — Volta-refined discriminator: "verify vs evaluate framing." Shape-D feedback comes from joint authors verifying the specific they have direct vantage on; folding the correction strengthens the entry's evidence base because the corrected content is now first-author-verified rather than drafter-inferred. The `[FLAG]` mechanism explicitly invites Shape-D corrections.

**Empirical instances (S35-S36)**:
- Volta 2.5 Sub-Instance 1 (2026-05-26): Cal's inferred "FR-design vs CF-canonical-doc" drift-class for Volta's lifecycle.md review was wrong; Volta's review actually caught FR-design-grade vs FR-design-exec-grade (INTERNAL to FR design, NESTED layer-pair). Path A (correction-in-place keeping n=4) over Path B (drop to n=3); Path A strengthened the entry because it provided evidence for "discipline applies at the layer above" at TWO layers up. Folded immediately as Path A.

**n=1 within S36**; the typology may eventually need a parallel sub-discipline for Shape-D corrections distinct from Shape-A/B/C framing-quality feedback per Volta-LEARNED observation.

### Shape-E — Axis-distinction discipline-check (cross-entry framing-coherence) — Herald-contribution

A Stage 2 proposal that **checks axis-distinction** across two or more wiki entries — specifically, that the framing of an entry's coupling-dimension or axis-of-decomposition stays consistent when the entry composes with sibling entries. Herald's S36 Sub-Instance 2 read-back surfaced this shape: the protocol-route layer's drift-class needed to be distinguished from the layer-pair drift-class used elsewhere in the cluster.

**Fold discipline**: **fold immediately, with cross-entry coherence-check**. The fold preserves the entry's claim-base AND strengthens cross-entry framing-coherence; sibling entries inherit the disambiguation through cross-references. n-threshold is not required because the fold operates on framing-coherence-as-evidence rather than claim-base-as-evidence.

**Empirical instances (S35-S36)**:
- Herald 2.5 Sub-Instance 2 (2026-05-26): protocol-route layer drift-class distinct from layer-pair drift-class; axis-distinction discipline-check across the substrate-truth-evidence cluster's framing. Folded immediately into Sub-Instance 2 specifics.

**n=1 within S36**; the cross-entry coherence-check is structurally a Shape-E because the discriminator question's answer is "preserves claim-base AND strengthens cross-entry framing-coherence" — distinct from Shape-A (within-entry renaming) and Shape-C (within-entry mechanism extension).

## Routing Table

For quick lookup during Stage 2 cycles:

| Shape | Claim-base | Mechanism | Epistemic locus | Fold discipline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Preserves | Preserves | Within-entry framing-quality | Fold immediately |
| **B** | Extends | Extends | Within-entry forward-claim | **Defer** to n=2 of meta-instances |
| **C** | Preserves | **Extends** | Within-entry mechanism explanation | Fold immediately with mechanism-labeling |
| **D** | Preserves (corrects inferred) | Preserves | Substantive-content verification (`[FLAG]`-invited) | Fold immediately; strengthens evidence base |
| **E** | Preserves | Preserves | Cross-entry framing-coherence | Fold immediately with cross-reference update |

The discriminator question (does the proposal preserve or extend claim-base?) is the **primary** routing fork (Shape-B path). The secondary fork distinguishes A/C/D/E on **what is being extended or verified** (nothing / mechanism / inferred-content / cross-entry coherence).

## Two Distinct Epistemic Layers (Volta-LEARNED 2026-05-26 16:05)

Stage 2 read-back operates on **two distinct epistemic layers**:

1. **Substantive-content verification layer** (Shape-D): "are the facts right?" — the drafter's inferences and `[FLAG]`-annotations are at this layer; Shape-D corrections live here.
2. **Framing-quality layer** (Shape-A/B/C/E): "is the framing of the verified facts right?" — sharpening, mechanism extension, claim-extension, cross-entry coherence all live here.

**Getting the facts right precedes getting the framing right.** When Stage 2 includes `[FLAG]` surfaces, substantive-content read-back is load-bearing in a way framing-quality read-back is not. The `[FLAG]` mechanism is the structural acknowledgment that reserves the substantive-content-verification slot ahead of the framing-quality surface.

This two-layer decomposition is itself a wiki-process candidate sub-discipline; the typology entry catalogs it as the structural observation that motivates Shape-D's distinct epistemic-layer placement.

## What This Is NOT

- **Not a closed set of shapes** — n=5 is current-state-comprehensive per Brunel family-completion guardrail; future Stage 2 cycles may surface Shape-F or Shape-G. Forward-watchpoint candidates: **"proceeding-to" as gate-slip signal** (S37 in-vivo: language-level discipline gap in status reports, distinct from content-level feedback; possibly Shape F or sub-shape of Shape E at language-layer); **L4-hold-as-response** (a Stage 2 proposal whose right response is explicit not-folding paired with explicit acknowledgment — distinct from defer because L4-hold is symmetric application, not waiting-for-evidence).
- **Not a substitute for the discriminator question** — the typology operationalizes the question; the question is canonical. New shapes route through the question first.
- **Not exhaustive of Stage 2 work** — Stage 2 ACK acknowledgment, Stage 2 author cross-confirmation, Stage 2 amendments-log construction all happen alongside the shape-routing; the typology covers the substantive content-fold decision, not the procedural-mechanics decision.
- **Not a one-time analysis** — typology grows with empirical surface area. The current shapes are S36-session-sampled; cross-session reproducibility is the promotion trigger.
