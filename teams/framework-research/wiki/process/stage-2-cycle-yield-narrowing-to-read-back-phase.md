# Stage-2 Cycle Yield Is at the Read-Back Phase, Not the Drafting Phase

The hypothesis that **joint Stage 2 read-back cycles have higher structural yield than solo Stage 2 cycles** (Volta sketch-grade, S36 C1 cycle) was tested across n=5 cumulative Stage 1 drafting instances over S36-S37 — n=5 entries drafted clean despite joint-source material. The empirical evidence narrows the hypothesis: **Stage 2 cycle structural-yield is at the read-back phase, NOT the drafting phase.** Stage 1 honest-fold is solo-author work even on joint-source material; joint-author contribution shows up at Stage 2 read-back, where multiple authors' vantages interact with the drafted artifact.

The distinction matters operationally: drafting strategy is **solo-author against committed source-of-truth** even for joint-sourced entries; coordination overhead in drafting is wasted unless the source-of-truth is not yet committed. Joint-author yield is **collected after drafting**, not during.

**Joint Callimachus + Aen** — Cal observed the narrowing empirically across drafting cycles; Aen ratified the framing at S36 15:26 + S36 15:20 endorsement of the narrowing as "strong meta-observation"; STANDING-WATCH established with promotion threshold n=3+ confirms or n=1 counter-instance disconfirms.

## The Hypothesis and Its Narrowing

**Original hypothesis (Volta S36 C1 cycle, sketch-grade)**: joint Stage 2 read-back cycles have higher structural yield than solo Stage 2 cycles because within-system self-applications are more visible at n=2 authors than at n=1.

**Volta's empirical falsifiability**: compare future joint vs solo Stage 2 cycle structural yields; n=2 of comparison required.

**Cal narrowing observation (S36 2.5 cycle close, 2026-05-26)**: the S6 hypothesis (joint yield > solo yield) **holds for the read-back phase but NOT for the drafting phase** — Stage 1 drafting was clean on a joint-source entry with 4 authors. This refines the hypothesis: the structural yield differential is specifically at the read-back-cycle phase, not the source-decomposition or drafting phase.

**Aen S36 15:26 endorsement**: "strong meta-observation"; possible future Cal entry candidate in its own right once n=3+ confirms or n=1 counter-instance disconfirms.

**Cross-cycle pattern**: each Stage 2 cycle produced substantive folds across 1-3 surfaces; the joint-author cohort interacting with the drafted artifact surfaces sub-findings, mechanism-sharpenings, attribution corrections, and family-completion guardrails that the solo-drafted artifact lacked.

**The yield-differential is at Stage 2**: solo drafting produces a complete-enough artifact for Stage 2 cohort interaction; cohort interaction surfaces structural enhancements at framing-quality and substantive-content layers (per [`stage-2-feedback-typology.md`](stage-2-feedback-typology.md) five shapes).

## Operational Consequence

When the source-of-truth is **already-committed** (scratchpads, ops-log, prior Aen relays), Stage 1 drafting **does not require joint-author coordination during the draft**. The solo-author drafts against the committed sources; coordination overhead is wasted unless the source is incomplete or under-committed.

When the source-of-truth is **not yet committed** (joint authors still working through substrate-truth before scratchpad-commit), Stage 1 drafting **does require coordination** — the drafter cannot fold from sources that don't yet exist.

**Discriminator question**: is the source-of-truth committed to scratchpad / ops-log / direct-DM artifact at drafting time? If YES, solo-author Stage 1 drafting; if NO, surface-back-for-context to joint authors before drafting OR wait for scratchpad commit.

The discriminator operationalizes the narrowing: source-commit-state determines drafting strategy; joint-author yield is reserved for Stage 2.

## Why Drafting Is Solo Even When Joint-Sourced

Three load-bearing reasons surfaced across the n=5 cumulative observations:

1. **Stage 1 honest-fold is fundamentally a source-reading operation** — the drafter reads committed sources + composes them against the entry's frame. Multiple drafters at the same source produce no additional yield (the source is the source); they duplicate work.
2. **FLAG annotations resolve mid-draft uncertainty** — when the drafter encounters an inferred specific that needs joint-author verification, the [FLAG] mechanism reserves the slot for Stage 2 correction. Mid-draft surface-back is unnecessary unless the FLAG density exceeds ~20-30% of substantive content (Brunel's S36 pre-emption on relay-thinness signal).
3. **Joint-author cohort interaction is structurally distinct from joint-author drafting** — read-back is *interaction with a complete artifact*; drafting is *construction of an artifact*. Different epistemic modes; read-back yields sub-findings that drafting cannot.

## Cross-Cycle Observation Pattern (Volta sketch-grade extension)

A parallel observation surfaced at S36 16:35 (Brunel secondary close-out) + 17:00 (Volta tertiary close-out): **within-system self-application moments surface as sub-observations within Stage 2 cycles** that include substantive feedback. n=2 within-cycle of within-system self-applications surfaced as sub-observations in a single Stage 2 read-back cycle (Brunel's "structurally elegant + self-validating" endorsement; Volta's recursive-validation moment observation on Stage-2-feedback-typology being self-applied).

**Sketch-grade pattern**: Stage 2 read-back cycles structurally surface within-system self-applications because the Stage 2 cycle itself is about discipline applying to its own outputs. Cousin-class to `first-use-recursive-validation.md` and `recursive-citation-as-canonical-validation.md`.

The within-system self-application yield is **also at Stage 2**, not Stage 1 — this is consistent with the S6 narrowing. The yield-narrowing applies to both substantive-content sub-findings AND within-system self-applications.

## What This Is NOT

- **Not "joint-source entries don't benefit from joint authors"** — joint-author yield is real; it lives at Stage 2, not Stage 1. Solo Stage 1 drafting against committed joint sources composes against the joint authors' upstream work; Stage 2 cohort interaction surfaces the additional yield.
- **Not "drafting is trivially solo"** — drafting against UNcommitted joint sources requires coordination. The discriminator (source-commit-state at drafting time) operationalizes the decision; ignoring source-commit-state and assuming solo drafting works always would over-extend the narrowing.
- **Not a substitute for Stage 2** — Stage 2 read-back is where joint-author yield lives; skipping Stage 2 because "drafting was clean" loses the structural enhancement. S6 says drafting strategy is solo; it does NOT say Stage 2 is optional.
- **Not framework-invariant yet** — n=5 within FR S36-S37 single-team session; cross-team confirmation distinguishes FR-discipline-culture from framework-invariant.
