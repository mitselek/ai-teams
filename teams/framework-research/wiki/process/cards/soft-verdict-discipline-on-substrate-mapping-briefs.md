## TLDR

When PO framing is in flight and team-lead asks for a surface map (not an architecture pick), deliver a table of N candidate options × {accommodates without modification / with additions only / requires replacement} with concrete code-cost per cell -- NOT a "recommended" verdict. Deliver the substrate's accommodation profile across all live candidates; let the architecture pick happen later.

## Key ideas

- **Why**: a recommended verdict commits early -- the PO either skips the architecture-pick step the brief wasn't authorized to make, or rejects it and the brief is now wrong-shaped (substrate facts bundled inside a rejected verdict).
- **Soft-verdict structurally separates substrate facts from architecture choice**: the accommodation profile is the same regardless of which shape gets picked.
- **The "what's free / what's not" rows live below the table**, independent of shape -- they describe the substrate's invariants.
- **In tension when**: PO has already picked the shape, the team is doing the pick itself, or a single invariant blocker disqualifies most shapes (lead with the blocker).
- **Signal for "PO framing in flight"**: 2+ reframing questions in a session, OR "map, don't pick" request, OR open variables in the problem statement.
- **Don't retrofit the map with the verdict** -- file the verdict in a follow-up; keep the map clean.
- **First instance**: Brilliant substrate map (#64); Finn re-framed his own Q7 mid-session, surviving PO's no-fallback walk-back. The meta-step (reframe before delivery) is what made the discipline visible.
