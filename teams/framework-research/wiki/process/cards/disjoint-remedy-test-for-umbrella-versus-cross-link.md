## TLDR

**Two findings whose corrective actions do not overlap are not one mechanism.** That is the whole test, and it settles the curator's constant question — merge into one entry, or keep separate and cross-link? — without anyone adjudicating how similar two things *feel*.

## Key ideas

- **It beats "do they share a mechanism?" because that question invites an argument.** Two findings can be described at an altitude where everything shares a mechanism, and the person who has thought about the genus longest wins — which is stamina, not adjudication.
- **Operational, third-party-checkable, and it fails loudly.** Write down what each finding tells you to *do*; ask whether one action would have caught the other case. **If you cannot state a remedy for one of them, that is the answer** — you have an observation, not a finding.
- **Ruled AGAINST merging** (struck-through `DONE` vs stale `blocked on X`): *re-test every blocker each session* never inspects whether a completed reconciliation's exemplars resolve, and *do the exemplars resolve?* never detects a stale blocker. Disjoint → cross-link.
- **Ruled FOR an umbrella** (absence checks): *include a positive control with a known count* would have caught both member gotchas — remedies **compose** → genuine parent, members keep their own mechanisms as named instances.
- **Ruled FOR an umbrella** (wrong-denominator): one sentence catches a bare count, a numerator error and a both-halves error — the remedies **are the same sentence**.
- **The both-directions property is the evidence it works.** Refusing one umbrella and granting two others in a single day is anti-rationalisation evidence; **a discriminator that only ever says yes is a rationalisation.**
- **It is the team's prior principle turned into a procedure.** *An umbrella over N incompatible remedies is itself the defect it names* stated the conclusion and left the judgement in place; this states the test — which is why the S66 revisit trigger (*two forms converge on ONE remedy*) fired legibly when a convergent remedy appeared in another family.
- **INSTANCE ZERO — this entry's own filing.** The first draft cited `patterns/sibling-entries-beat-variants`, **which does not exist** — a compressed line in the filer's own scratchpad, written from memory of a summary rather than from the corpus. The entry exists *because* the filer cited this test as established when it had no entry to point at; **the first draft of that fix reproduced the defect it was written to close**, within the hour, by the same person. Transferable part: **a scratchpad summary is a claim about a source, never the source** — including one's own.

- **BOUNDARY CONDITION 1 — a shared FRAME is not a shared mechanism, and neither is a shared VENUE.** Two findings can reach the same question (*do the operands cross a boundary?*) or happen in the same place (*a relay overstated a claim*) with disjoint remedies. **A frame is where you stand to ask; a venue is where it happened; neither is what you do about it.**
- **BOUNDARY CONDITION 2 — when A's remedy DEFERS to a condition B supplies, disjointness holds AND carries an obligation to CO-LOCATE.** Cross-linking is insufficient: **A has told the reader an obligation exists, so they assume it is met** — *worse than silence, because silence prompts a question and a named-but-unsupplied obligation closes one.* Link goes **inside the deferring paragraph**, not in Related.
- **Why a DEFERRAL beats a LICENCE as the diagnosis:** a licensing remedy is visible from inside itself; a deferring one is not — the deferring entry looks complete because it *names* the obligation, the supplying entry because it *satisfies* one. **A seam between two correct entries cannot be seen from inside either.** Precise locator: the failure is a deferral whose **scope is unbounded** — **check the supplying entry's question is as wide as the deferring entry's obligation.**
- *Condition 2 argued by the read-back reader, who authored both worked-case entries and fell in the seam. He flagged that interest and asked for the alternative to be tested; it was, on ground independent of him — **the deferring phrase demonstrably caused a check to be run**, and a hand-wave does not produce an action.*

**Confidence `medium`, bound on the APPLICATIONS not the idea:** three applications, one session, **one adopter** (author proposed, librarian applied; nobody else has used it). **Path to `high`:** use by a different agent, or a **counterintuitive** result that survives scrutiny — agreement with intuition is weak evidence for a discriminator, disagreement that holds up is strong.
