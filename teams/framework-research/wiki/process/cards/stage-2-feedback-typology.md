## TLDR

Joint authors' Stage 2 read-back responses on Protocol A entries cluster into five distinct epistemic shapes, each requiring a different fold discipline. The canonical discriminator (Volta): "Does the proposal preserve the existing claim-base or extend it?" Preserve = fold immediately; extend = defer to n=2.

## Key ideas

- **Shape-A (pure renaming)**: sharpens existing label without changing claims. Fold immediately. n=3 in S36.
- **Shape-B (forward-claim-extension)**: extends claim-base into adjacent territory at current n-count. Defer per methodology corollary -- do not lean harder at n=1. n=2 in S36.
- **Shape-C (mechanism-sharpening)**: preserves claim-base, extends the "why" explanation. Fold immediately with mechanism-labeling. n=1 in S36.
- **Shape-D (factual-correction on FLAG)**: corrects drafter-inferred content where FLAG invited correction. Fold immediately -- strengthens evidence base. Distinct epistemic layer from A/B/C/E.
- **Shape-E (axis-distinction)**: checks cross-entry framing-coherence on coupling-dimensions. Fold immediately with cross-reference update. n=1 in S36.
- **Two epistemic layers**: substantive-content verification (Shape-D, "are the facts right?") precedes framing-quality (A/B/C/E, "is the framing right?"). FLAG mechanism reserves the substantive slot.
- **Not a closed set**: Shape-F ("proceeding-to" as gate-slip) and Shape-G (L4-hold-as-response) are forward-watchpoints. Cross-cycle n=2 promotes to confidence-high.
